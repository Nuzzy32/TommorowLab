# Ambient sound credits

Real field and studio recordings used by the 3D canteen (`src/scene/canteen.ts`). Each file was cut, converted to
mono, loudness-matched and encoded as 96 kbps MP3. Loops were given a 1.5–2 s equal-power crossfade at the seam.
